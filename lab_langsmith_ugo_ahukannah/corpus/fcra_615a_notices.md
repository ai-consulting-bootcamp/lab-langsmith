# FCRA §615 — Adverse Action Disclosures (working reference)

*Cardinal Trust Bank compliance handbook, Part 2. Authored for training use.*

**FC-1 — When §615(a) applies.**
Where adverse action is taken with respect to a consumer that is based in whole or
in part on information contained in a **consumer report**, the disclosures in FC-2
through FC-5 must be provided.

**FC-2 — Identifying the consumer reporting agency.**
The notice must give the name, address and telephone number of the consumer
reporting agency that furnished the report. For a nationwide agency, the telephone
number given must be a **toll-free** number established by that agency.

**FC-3 — The agency did not decide.**
The notice must state that the consumer reporting agency **did not make the
decision** to take the adverse action and **is unable to provide the specific
reasons** for it.

**FC-4 — Right to a free copy of the report.**
The consumer must be told of their right to obtain a free copy of the consumer
report from the agency, and the request must be made within **60 days** of
receiving the adverse action notice.

**FC-5 — Right to dispute.**
The consumer must be told of their right to dispute directly with the consumer
reporting agency the accuracy or completeness of any information in the report.

**FC-6 — Credit score disclosure.**
Where a **numerical credit score** was used in taking the adverse action, the
notice must additionally disclose: the score itself; the range of possible scores
under the model used; the key factors that adversely affected the score, listed in
order of importance, **up to four** — or **up to five** where the number of
enquiries on the file was one of the key factors; the date the score was created;
and the name of the person or entity that provided the score.

**FC-7 — Information from third parties that are not consumer reporting agencies.**
Where adverse action is based in whole or in part on information obtained from a
third party that is **not** a consumer reporting agency, the consumer must be told
of their right to learn the **nature** of that information, and that request must
be made within **60 days** of learning of the adverse action. The creditor is not
required to disclose the source of the information.
