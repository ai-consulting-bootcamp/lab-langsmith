# Northstar Outfitters Customer Support Policy (v1)

This fictional policy is the source document for the lab's evaluation dataset. It is intentionally small, self-contained, and versioned so that the reference answers can be audited.

## Returns and exchanges

- Unopened, unused items may be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery for a full refund to the original payment method.
- Opened items may be returned within 14 calendar days of delivery only if they are defective or damaged on arrival. A photo of the defect is required.
- Sale items are exchange-only within 14 calendar days; they are not eligible for a cash refund.
- Personalized items and gift cards are final sale and cannot be returned or exchanged.
- The customer pays return shipping for a change-of-mind return. Northstar pays return shipping for defective or damaged items.
- Refunds are issued within 5 business days after the returned item passes inspection. The bank may take an additional 3–5 business days to display the refund.

## Shipping

- Standard shipping costs $6 and usually arrives in 3–5 business days.
- Express shipping costs $18 and usually arrives in 1–2 business days.
- Orders over $75 qualify for free standard shipping. Express surcharges still apply.
- An order may be canceled for a full refund before it ships. After shipment, the customer must use the returns process.

## Warranty and support

- Manufacturing defects are covered for 12 months after delivery. Normal wear, accidental damage, and misuse are not covered.
- Warranty claims require the order number and a description of the problem; photos or a short video may be requested.
- Support hours are Monday–Friday, 09:00–17:00 Central European Time. Typical response time is two business days.
- The policy does not promise Saturday delivery, price matching, or refunds for late carrier delivery.
