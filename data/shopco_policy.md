# ShopCo — Customer Support Policy
Version: 1.0

This is a fictional policy created for this learning exercise.
It does not describe a real company.

## Shipping
S1. ShopCo delivers only to mainland Spain.
S2. Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days after dispatch.
S3. Standard shipping costs €4. Orders of €50 or more have free shipping.
S4. Customers receive a tracking link by email after dispatch.
S5. If an order has not arrived after 5 business days from dispatch,
the customer should contact customer support with the order number.

## Returns
R1. Customers can request a return within 30 calendar days of delivery.
R2. For a change-of-mind return, the product must be unused,
in its original packaging, and accompanied by proof of purchase.
R3. Sale items follow the same return rules as full-price items.
R4. For an eligible change-of-mind return, the customer pays
the return shipping cost.
R5. For a damaged or incorrect product, the customer should contact
support with the order number and a photo. ShopCo pays the return
shipping cost.
R6. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within
7 business days after ShopCo receives and approves the return.

## Support and missing information
C1. Customers can contact support at support@shopco.example.
C2. If this policy does not contain the answer, the assistant must
say that the information is not available and refer the customer
to support. It must not invent rules.
C3. If the customer's information is insufficient to apply a rule,
the assistant must ask for the missing details before confirming
eligibility.
