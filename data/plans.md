# CloudDesk plans, version 1

CloudDesk is a fictional workspace SaaS used for a controlled support evaluation. All prices and policies in this corpus are synthetic, not commercial offers.

## PLAN-01: Plans and billing
Free supports at most 3 members and 1 GB of workspace storage. Free has no API access.
Team costs USD 12 per billable seat per month on monthly billing, or USD 120 per billable seat per year on annual billing. Team has a minimum of 5 billable seats, 100 GB of workspace storage, and API access.
Business costs USD 25 per billable seat per month on monthly billing, or USD 240 per billable seat per year on annual billing. Business has a minimum of 10 billable seats, 1 TB of workspace storage, API access, and SAML SSO.
Annual prices are charged upfront for the full year. Prices exclude taxes. A member occupies a billable seat; a read-only guest does not. Minimum billable seats still apply even when there are fewer members.

## PLAN-02: Plan changes
An upgrade takes effect immediately and the unused portion of the old plan is credited against the prorated charge for the new plan. A downgrade takes effect at the end of the current billing period. Removing a member does not refund the current period; the next renewal uses the new member count, subject to the plan minimum.
