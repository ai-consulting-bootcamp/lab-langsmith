# CloudDesk billing policy, version 1

## BILL-01: Cancellation and refunds
The workspace owner can cancel renewal in Settings > Billing. Cancellation prevents the next renewal and paid features remain available until the current paid period ends. Cancellation does not delete workspace data.
A refund is available only for the first paid subscription charge when the owner submits a request within 14 elapsed days (336 hours) of that charge, inclusive. This applies to monthly and annual subscriptions. Renewal charges, add-ons, and requests after 336 hours are not eligible under the standard policy. Canceling renewal does not itself submit a refund request. Refund requests go to billing@clouddesk.example with the workspace ID and invoice number. An approved refund returns to the original payment method in 5-10 business days. The assistant can explain eligibility but cannot approve, submit, or execute refunds.

## BILL-02: Invoices and tax
Owners and billing admins can download invoices from Settings > Billing > Invoices. Only the owner can cancel renewal. Tax identification changes apply to future invoices. Previously issued invoices cannot be edited through self-service; billing support must review them. No tax rate is specified in this document.
