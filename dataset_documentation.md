## Part 1: Setup & Document Selection

### Step 1: Choose Your Domain and Documents

**Domain:** B2B/SaaS marketing operations — lead qualification and conversion prediction. Given a lead's 30-day engagement snapshot (firmographics, lead source/channel, touch and session activity, estimated deal value), the task is to predict whether the lead converts to a paying customer within 90 days. This mirrors a core marketing-ops function: scoring and qualifying leads by channel (marketing-sourced vs. SDR outbound vs. partner referral) to prioritize sales follow-up and report marketing's contribution to pipeline.

**Source(s):** [LeadForge Lead Scoring: Intro (v1)](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/derelictpanda/leadforge-lead-scoring-intro-v1) on Kaggle. 5,000 B2B SaaS lead records for a fictional procurement/AP automation vendor (Veridian Technologies), MIT licensed. Fully synthetic but purpose-built and documented Generated by the open-source `leadforge` framework with a fixed, disclosed recipe (`b2b_saas_procurement_v1`, seed 42) and published baseline metrics.

**What makes a good example:**
- Input: one lead's 30-day snapshot with firmographics (industry, region, employee band, revenue band), `lead_source` (inbound_marketing, sdr_outbound, partner_referral), engagement signals (touch_count, session_count, pricing/demo page views, days since first/last touch), and `opportunity_estimated_acv`.
- Output: ground-truth `converted_within_90_days` (True/False).
- Good examples vary across lead source, industry, and engagement level, so the model has to weigh the actual signals rather than guess the majority class (~43% conversion rate in this tier).
