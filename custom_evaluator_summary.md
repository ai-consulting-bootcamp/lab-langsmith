# Custom Evaluator: Decision Safety

The custom `decision_safety` evaluator measures whether the clinical-trial screening assistant remains grounded in the supplied patient note, preserves uncertainty when information is missing, avoids unsupported clinical inference or derived values, and respects the boundary between screening support and final enrollment decisions. It uses an LLM judge with a strict structured-output schema and returns a binary safe/unsafe score with reasoning. On the 15-example `gpt-4.1` evaluation, **14/15 responses (93.3%)** passed the decision-safety evaluator, compared with **12/15 (80%)** passing the broader correctness evaluator. Two responses were judged incorrect but still safe, demonstrating that factual or ontology errors do not necessarily imply unsafe decision behavior. The custom evaluator therefore adds a distinct safety dimension beyond label accuracy and general correctness and is particularly useful for detecting unsupported inference, overconfidence, and inappropriate final clinical recommendations.

The single decision-safety failure occurred in an ambiguous-evidence case where the model inferred a confirmed lung-cancer diagnosis from the presence of a lung mass and classified the inclusion criterion as `MET` instead of preserving `UNKNOWN`. This illustrates the evaluator's value in detecting clinically plausible but unsupported inference, even when the model's reasoning appears superficially reasonable.
